📋 Intended Learning Outcomes (ILOs)

This physical Rube Goldberg simulation is engineered to achieve the following educational standards:

  1. 1
    Static vs. Kinetic Friction Thresholds Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given two materials with different static and kinetic coefficients of friction, the student will calculate the maximum static friction force ($F_{s,\max}$) versus kinetic friction force ($F_k$) to correctly predict whether the object transitions from a stationary rest state into motion.
  2. 2
    Inclined Plane Vector Deconstruction Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given a variable ramp slope angle and a set gravitational constant, the student will calculate the parallel ($F_{g,\parallel}$) and perpendicular ($F_N$) force components to verify how normal force declines as inclination increases.
  3. 3
    Normal Force Dependencies Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given the transition of a sliding container from an inclined ramp to a flat horizontal runway, the student will describe the changes in the normal force vector ($F_N$) to explain the resulting step-up in kinetic deceleration drag.
  4. 4
    Energy & Momentum Transfer Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given an inelastic mechanical collision scenario within a multi-stage mechanism, the student will calculate the incoming projectile's momentum and terminal velocity to determine whether the mechanical target receives sufficient kinetic energy to activate its trigger switch.
  5. 5
    Constraint Optimization Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given a strict target-zone target location, the student will select a balanced combination of inclined materials and deck friction coefficients to bring the sliding object to a safe, complete stop directly inside the success zone without overshooting or undershooting.
  6. 6
    Iterative Troubleshooting & Debugging Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given a mechanical alignment failure in any level of the chain reaction, the student will isolate and adjust individual material values and slope angles to systematically solve the physical bottleneck in fewer than five trial runs.
  7. 7
    Algebraic Inequality Assessment Formula: [Condition] + [Audience] + [Action Verb/Behavior] + [Degree] Given a live equations chalkboard, the student will evaluate real-time algebraic inequalities of static friction ($F_{g,\parallel} \le \mu_s F_N$) to verify mathematically why an object remains locked at rest.
